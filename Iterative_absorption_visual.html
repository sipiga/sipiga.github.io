Iterative absorption: the cover-down step

To split a graph into triangles, iterative absorption pushes the uncovered edges into smaller and smaller sets of vertices, until so few vertices remain that an absorber prepared in advance can finish the job. Press Play to watch it happen, and Stop when you think the leftover is small enough.
